What to spend Glimpses on

Glimpses look like a minor currency for the first few hours. They are the bottleneck on one of the longest achievement chains in the game, and most guides mention them in a single clause.

The one thing not to buy

You can spend Glimpses in the Shellkeeper's room to mark the location of Shells you have not found. Do not.Tested You will find them all anyway — none are missable, and all eight can be collected after the final boss during free roam.Tested

Where they should go instead

Shell Bond. Bond level is what unlocks Shell Memories,Tested and watching every Memory is required for the Seeking the Past achievement.Tested

Here is the part that reframes the whole budget: there are not enough Glimpses in a single playthrough to see every Memory. The earliest anyone can complete Seeking the Past is a second New Game Plus cycle, and it is likely to be among the last achievements you earn.Tested

How to budget a first run

Because the achievement is multi-cycle by design, the first run is not where you optimise for completion. It is where you avoid waste.

  • Do not spread thin. Deepening one or two Shells you actually use beats topping up five you do not.
  • Cleanse Beacons. Beacon dungeons pay out Ova plus resources and Glimpse, so they are the main faucet.Unconfirmed
  • Leave Bond tiers unfinished on purpose. Bond progress carries into New Game Plus, and the next cycle's Glimpse flow is where you finish them.Unconfirmed
Disputed

One New Game Plus guide flags an exception: a specific NPC's Bond interaction breaks in the next cycle, so that one has to be pushed to its top tier during the first run. We have seen this in a single source and have not cross-checked it. If you are Bond-farming that character, verify before assuming you can defer.
