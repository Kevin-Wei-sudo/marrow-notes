Does beta progress carry over?

No. Your save does not transfer. You keep one cosmetic and, if you got far enough, the option to skip the prologue. Everything else resets.

What the store listings actually say

To preserve the balance of the full release, beta save progress does not carry over in full. Currency, weapons, Shells, collectibles and other items found during the open beta reset at launch.Official

Two things survive:

  • The Flayed Harbinger, an exclusive cosmetic unlocked by playing the open beta, claimable in the full game.Official
  • A prologue skip, unlocked if you progressed beyond the Marrow Keep in the beta.Official

The retail game and the open beta are separate store applications. On Steam the retail app is 2584270 and the beta is 4711740.Unconfirmed

Where the prologue skip drops you

Taking the skip starts you at your first visit to the Keep, which is just after the Tar Golem fight.Tested You lose nothing by skipping, because there is nothing to pick up in the prologue.Tested

What you definitely lose

Everything collected in the Mushroom Village — items, Tarstones and the Mushroom Village key included.Tested Do not farm beta resources on the assumption that inventory transfers. The reset is deliberate.Official

Why you have seen the opposite claim

Disputed

At least one outlet published this news under a headline stating that beta progress carries over to the full game, which is the reverse of what the store listings say. Separately, a site we do not link claims the beta rewards include additional shades and a custom parry animation effect — no official listing mentions these. If a source describes beta rewards beyond the Flayed Harbinger and the prologue skip, it is not reading the store page.

Editions, briefly

The Standard Edition released worldwide on 20 August 2026.Official The Devout Edition included up to 72 hours of advanced accessOfficial plus an Obsidian Skin Set covering the eight playable Shells.Unconfirmed A physical Revered Edition is PlayStation 5 exclusive.Unconfirmed

Cold Symmetry never announced an official closing date for the open beta.Unconfirmed
